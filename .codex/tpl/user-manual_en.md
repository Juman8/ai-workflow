## Introduction

This manual explains how to operate the ○○ system.

### Target Audience

- General users responsible for ○○ operations.

### Prerequisites

- An internet browser (Chrome / Edge recommended) must be installed.
- An account must have been issued by the system administrator.

---

## 1. Login / Logout

### 1-1. Login

1. Open the following URL in your browser:
   `https://○○.example.com`
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Click the **Login** button.
4. Login is complete when the dashboard screen appears.

> **Note:** Your account will be locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. If locked, please contact your administrator.

### 1-2. Logout

1. Click your username in the upper right corner of the screen.
2. Select **Logout**.

---

## 2. ○○ List Screen

### 2-1. Viewing the List

1. Click **○○ Management** in the left menu.
2. The ○○ list will be displayed.
3. 20 items are shown per page.

### 2-2. Searching & Filtering

1. Enter a keyword in the search bar.
2. Click the **Search** button.
3. Matching records will appear in the list.

> Search keywords use partial matching.

### 2-3. Viewing Details

1. Click a row in the list.
2. The detail screen will be displayed.

---

## 3. ○○ Registration

### 3-1. New Registration

1. Click the **New Registration** button on the list screen.
2. Fill in each field.

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| ○○ Name | ✓ | Max 50 characters |
| ○○ Category | ✓ | Select from dropdown |
| Notes | - | Max 200 characters |

3. Click the **Register** button.
4. Registration is complete when "Registration complete" is displayed.

### 3-2. Editing

1. Click the **Edit** button on the target row in the list screen.
2. Make changes and click the **Update** button.

### 3-3. Deleting

1. Click the **Delete** button on the target row in the list screen.
2. Click **OK** in the confirmation dialog.

> **Note:** Deleted data cannot be restored.

---

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q. What should I do if I forget my password?**
A. Click "Forgot your password?" on the login screen.

**Q. An error is displayed and I cannot proceed.**
A. Reload the page and try again. If the issue persists, please contact your administrator.
